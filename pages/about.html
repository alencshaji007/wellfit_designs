About Us

Creating Spaces That Are Beautiful, Functional and Built to Last.

Wellfit Designs is the operational brand of WELL FIT SPACE DECORATION DESIGN & IMPLEMENTATION L.L.C., officially established in Dubai in 2025. We work from our Dubai headquarters, supported by a production facility in Ajman, and deliver interior solutions across the UAE with attention to detail.

Who We Are

We work hand in hand with clients, asking the right questions, understanding their needs and delivering spaces where people can truly enjoy their work and their lives.

Our Story

Our journey began in 2020.

  1. 2020

    Our journey began in 2020 with JAN INTERIOR FIT OUT, an interior design and fit-out business in the UAE supported by an in-house joinery factory in Ajman, known for craftsmanship and design expertise.

  2. 2025

    In 2025, we restructured our operations to create a more focused and coordinated business model. Wellfit Designs now manages project operations from Dubai, covering design development through complete turnkey execution, while JAN Interiors focuses exclusively on joinery production.

This structure connects design, planning, production and on-site execution, helping us deliver coordinated interior solutions tailored to each project’s requirements, with less time spent organising and more time designing and collaborating with you.

Our Strength

Dubai project operations, Ajman joinery production.

Our operating model brings together Dubai-based project operations and dedicated joinery production in Ajman. This connection allows us to coordinate bespoke joinery requirements with the wider interior design and fit-out scope.

At every stage, our focus remains on understanding the client, making practical design decisions and coordinating the work required to achieve the intended result.
