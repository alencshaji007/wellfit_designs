Our Process

From Your Vision to Your Finished Space

Every project is different. We tailor the process to the property’s requirements, the agreed scope and any applicable authority or building-management procedures.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We understand your requirements, preferences, budget and objectives.

  2. 02

    Design

    Layouts, concepts and material directions tailored to the space.

  3. 03

    Define

    Agreed scope, specifications, quotation and execution plan.

  4. 04

    Coordinate

    Approvals, technical details, suppliers, joinery and schedules.

  5. 05

    Execute

    Managed site works and coordinated trades.

  6. 06

    Handover

    Final review against the agreed scope.
